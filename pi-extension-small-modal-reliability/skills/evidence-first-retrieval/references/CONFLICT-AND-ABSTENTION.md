# Conflict handling and abstention

Conflicting passages are evidence about uncertainty, not an invitation to select the most convenient answer.

## Record the conflict

For each material claim, keep supporting and contradicting passages separate. A conflict is unresolved until one of these dispositions is explicit:

- **Prefer source:** identify the preferred cited source and explain the bounded reason, such as authority, date, or directness.
- **Report conflict:** describe the disagreement in the answer without claiming it has been settled.
- **Exclude claim:** omit the unsupported or contested claim from the answer.
- **Escalate:** request the decision or additional evidence needed to proceed safely.

A disposition makes the uncertainty auditable. It does not make a contested proposition true.

## Choose an honest outcome

- **supported:** material claims have resolving citations and no explicit freshness constraint remains unmet.
- **partial:** some useful result is available but a non-final limitation, such as a freshness concern, remains visible.
- **conflicting:** sources disagree and the answer must communicate that disagreement or request a decision.
- **insufficient:** a material claim has no resolving support, a source is missing, or the entity cannot be located.

Use `insufficient` for nonexistent entities and missing sources. Do not fabricate a citation, create an uncited fallback, or infer a missing fact from source authority alone.
